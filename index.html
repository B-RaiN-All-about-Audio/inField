How inField Works With Your Existing Phone:

Ordinary iOS/Android apps cannot read missed calls and send background SMS directly from your personal SIM. Instead, inField uses a dedicated Twilio business number ((910) 335-8490) that forwards calls to your cell ((910) 787-2450) or catches unanswered calls via carrier Conditional Call Forwarding (*71).

Voicemail & Carrier Note: Set your ring timeout (15–18s) shorter than your carrier voicemail pickup (~25s), and complete US A2P 10DLC registration for live carrier SMS delivery.
